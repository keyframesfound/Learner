Why Hong Kong is humid in summer
⋮⋮ Note
In summer the south-west monsoon carries warm, moist air from the Indian Ocean and the South China Sea. Because the air is warm it can hold a lot of water vapour, so relative humidity stays above 80% for most of June to August.
