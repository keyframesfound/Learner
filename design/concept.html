DDR4 vs DDR5 memory

RAM is the workbench. Everything the CPU is running right now sits on it, which is why how much you have decides how many programs stay open at once.

DDR4DDR5
Speed2133–32004800–6400
Voltage1.2 V1.1 V
Per stick32 GB128 GB

DDR5 is not simply a faster DDR4 — one 64-bit channel is split into two 32-bit halves, so it can answer two requests at once instead of one after the other.

What the exam asks

They are physically incompatible. The notch sits in a different place, so a DDR5 stick will not go into a DDR4 slot — the generation has to match the motherboard.

The speeds above are the common range from the JEDEC standard, not the fastest sticks sold. A number outside the range is an overclock, which is a different question.
